sequenceDiagram
    participant M as Mota (Nodo)
    participant R as Router

    Note over M: Usuario inicia "Buscar Red"

    loop Barrido de canales
        Note over M: Cambia frecuencia (Salto de canal)
        M->>+R: BEACON_REQUEST (0x00)
        Note right of M: Payload: Vacío

        Note right of R: Recibe solicitud
        Note right of R: Espera Jitter aleatorio (50-200ms)

        R-->>-M: BEACON_RESPONSE (0x01)
        Note right of M: Payload: struct NetworkData<br/>(SSID: "Finca_Norte", RouterID: 10)

        Note over M: Guarda Red en lista temporal
    end

    Note over M: Muestra lista en OLED
